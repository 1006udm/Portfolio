    Writing from the margins

    Marginalia is a platform that helps readers turn their notes and highlights from books into organized, shareable insights. It combines the depth of personal note-taking with the reach of public publishing. Users can capture and summarize ideas, keep them private as part of their own learning archive, or share them as thoughtful reflections for others to explore.
